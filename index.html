About

I am a master's student in Computer Engineering at Columbia University, where I work on robot learning in Prof. Yunzhu Li's group. I am interested in making simulation and learned world models trustworthy enough to train and evaluate real robots, and in training LLM agents that improve themselves through verifiable feedback. I also collaborate remotely with Prof. Philip S. Yu at UIC on agentic reinforcement learning.

Before Columbia, I completed a dual-degree program at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (B.S.) and Zhejiang University (B.E.), where I worked with Prof. Hanghang Tong on LLM reasoning over knowledge graphs and with Prof. Gaoang Wang on personalized image captioning.

Next steps. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 in robot learning and embodied AI. If my work looks relevant to your group, I would love to hear from you: jz4019@columbia.edu.

Research Interests

  • World models and Sim2Real. Augmenting simulators with learned style-transfer models so that they serve both as faithful evaluators and as scalable data engines for policy learning.
  • Robot learning. Imitation learning, RL post-training, and on-policy distillation for manipulation policies, including VLA models and dexterous grasping.
  • Agentic RL and self-evolving agents. Verifiable rewards for multi-turn tool use, and agents that autonomously build and verify their own skills.

Project Highlights

Augmenting Simulation with World Models for Sim2Real Style Transfer ongoing

Lead author · Prof. Yunzhu Li, Columbia · Jun 2026 – present

A simulator augmented by a style-transfer world model can act both as a trustworthy evaluator and as a scalable training-data engine. On a 2.5 mm-tolerance gear-assembly task (MuJoCo, Franka FR3), policy success in augmented sim correlates with real-robot success at Pearson 0.93. A curriculum-PPO teacher with augmented on-policy distillation reaches ~100% success in sim-only training. Style-transfer inference on Cosmos 3 Nano was accelerated 8.4× (2.8 s to 0.33 s per frame on GH200) via DMD2 distillation, context parallelism, and hierarchical sparse attention.

Interactive World Model for VLA Imitation Learning and RL Post-Training

Research assistant · Prof. Yunzhu Li, Columbia · Feb – May 2026

A task-specific interactive world model trained on ~4 hours of real-robot data rolls out physically consistent, 10+ minute trajectories at 15 FPS on one RTX 4090. I designed a closed-loop data engine around it (imitation, MPPI warm-starts, replaying RL failures on the real robot), post-trained Diffusion Policy with SAC using VLM-logit rewards, and quantified the model's fidelity as a policy evaluator against real-world rollouts.

DARPA TIAMAT Challenge: Sim-to-Sim Transfer for Boston Dynamics Spot

Perception lead · Columbia team with Prof. Yunzhu Li · Dec 2025 – Feb 2026

Train a Spot in low-fidelity Habitat-Sim, then transfer within a fixed budget to an unseen high-fidelity Isaac Sim. I owned the perception module: three domain-randomization pipelines, YOLO-World fine-tuning studies, and a real-time open-set detection dual system (YOLO-World proposals verified by DINOv2) reaching 0.97 precision on unseen objects, with round-trip latency cut from 780 ms to 620 ms by overlapping DINOv2 precompute with YOLO inference.

FuncGrasp: Semantically-Guided Functional Dexterous Grasping

Course project · Prof. Yunzhu Li, Columbia · Aug – Dec 2025

A VLM + diffusion stack for the Shadow Hand: a LoRA-tuned Qwen2-VL-2B maps language, RGB-D, point clouds (PointTransformer), and state to per-subtask contact maps, and a DDPM conditioned on keyframe contact maps predicts 34-D action chunks. Extended DexGraspNet 1.0 with affordance maps and multi-view images in MuJoCo and added collision losses, improving success and semantic following by ~31%.

CoEvoSkills: Self-Evolving Agent Skills via Co-Evolutionary Verification COLM 2026

With Prof. Philip S. Yu, UIC · Jan – Apr 2026

A Skill Generator and an information-isolated Surrogate Verifier co-evolve through generate–verify–refine cycles to build multi-file agent skills without access to ground-truth tests. On SkillsBench the evolved skills reach a 71.1% pass rate (+40.5pp over no skills, +17.6pp over human-curated skills), and skills evolved by one model transfer to six other LLMs with +35–44pp gains.

ToolTurn: Multi-Turn Tool-Use Agent RL with Graph-Guided Data Synthesis IEEE BigData 2026

With Prof. Philip S. Yu, UIC · Aug 2025 – present

A Tool-Dependency Graph backend synthesizes and filters multi-turn tool-call trajectories across 14 benchmark tasks, and doubles as a sandbox that emits per-turn verifiable rewards from state matching. Qwen2-7B and Qwen3-8B trained with SFT followed by GRPO/DAPO and curriculum learning gain ~10% over the SFT baseline on BFCL and API-Bank multi-turn.

VR Teleoperation of a UR3e Arm for Object Grasping

Senior design · Prof. Liangjing Yang, Zhejiang University · Jan – Jun 2025

Real-time remote control (latency under 33 ms, ~20 m range) of a UR3e through a Unity digital twin and a Meta Quest 3S interface. A 3D-printed PLA + TPU gripper closes the loop on contact force with an STM32 + CAN controller on a custom PCB. Open-sourced and reproducible.

EmbRAG: LLM Reasoning over Incomplete Temporal Knowledge Graphs

Research assistant · Prof. Hanghang Tong, UIUC · Sep 2023 – Jan 2025

Rules are sampled by random walk and scored by hit frequency and temporal association; a decoder generates new rules from high-confidence prompts. Fuzzy logic and rule-embedding similarity fill missing adjacency entries, which enables end-to-end LoRA fine-tuning of the LLM and reaches state-of-the-art results on multiple temporal knowledge-graph benchmarks.

Knowledge graphsLLM reasoningLoRA

Personalized Image Captioning for Social Platforms PRCV 2023

Undergraduate group leader · Prof. Gaoang Wang, Zhejiang University · Jan 2022 – Jul 2023

User-specific cues are encoded as a fixed-length query prefix projected into CLIP's visual feature space and injected into GPT-2's key-value layers; the mapping network is trained end to end. Doubled BLEU-4 and other caption-quality metrics over baselines on Instagram-style datasets.
